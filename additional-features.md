## Additional Features And Improvements.

When the total number of students is out of capacity for a room, the system should have another option for allocating a room and that room should also have invigilators.
     
A student should be able to download and print their own QR Code also in bulk like all the course units they have to do at once for example cybersecurity, computer repair, networking, data communication etc.
     
Students who did not show up at all for the paper should also be on the admin dashboard
     
The system should allow invigilators to give their overall remarks concerning a particular exam when everything or the days session is done or completed.
     
Total list of Active Sessions, Total Students, Total Present and Total Late should be clickable to show or reveal the full list of those students and their details.
     
Add/ There should be signing out using the QR Code after submitting the paper or when the exam completed by the student to confirm they submitted their completed exam, not the system only storing their sign in or signing in to begin exam.
     
The system should enable or have percentage processes of the examination, that is, when a student signs in for the exam it shows 50% after finishing and signing out becomes 100%.
